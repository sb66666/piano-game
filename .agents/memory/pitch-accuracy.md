Piano pitch detection must prioritize accuracy. Skipping the Central Do exercise is navigation to the next interface, not a simulated detection or completion. After actual Central Do success, continue to a hand map showing right-hand thumb=Do, index=Re, middle=Mi, ring=Fa, little finger=Sol.

**Why:** the user explicitly asked for accurate pitch and corrected the meaning of the skip control.

**How to apply:** use the standard A4=440 Hz pitch reference, test note identification and tolerance boundaries, keep skip distinct from live detection, and preserve the stated finger-to-note mapping in lesson progression.
